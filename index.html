Marketing · Content client

LinkedIn Content Assistant

I built the client a way to write, approve and schedule LinkedIn posts from a Telegram chat: three n8n workflows with 41 steps.

  • The client sends a message like "post about AI agents on Friday at 10". Claude pulls out the topic and time and writes a draft with sources.
  • The client approves, rejects or asks for changes in Telegram, and Claude rewrites it.
  • Approved posts go out on time. If publishing fails, the client gets a message.
What changedThe client plans, approves and publishes LinkedIn posts from Telegram, without opening LinkedIn.

  • Claude API
